How the model works

Brightness enters the graph

The model samples a horizontal pattern at published eye columns. Positive brightness changes enter L1 and negative changes enter L2.

Wiring shapes the activity

FlyWire synapse counts weight connections to eight intermediate cell types. The model combines faster and slower signals at T4 and T5, then averages each subtype for the readout.

The readout drives steering

A linear decoder learns direction from 48 calibration scenes. Its weights stay fixed across the controls. The simulated controller converts the per-frame readout into a bounded steering correction.

Controls test contributions

Shuffles and silencing ask which parts of this model contribute to performance. The pixel correlator provides a brightness-based reference. Compare across seeds and patches before attributing an effect to the wiring.
